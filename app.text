HaproID (by Haproven) ek digital identity profile platform hai. Ye users ko online apni professional pehchan (digital identity) banane aur share karne me madad karta hai.
Key Features
 * All-in-One Profile: Aap apni basic info (About me), Skills, Projects (Portfolio), aur Social Media links sabhi ko ek hi jagah add kar sakte hain.
 * No Coding Needed: Profile banane ke liye kisi coding, hosting ya domain ki zaroorat nahi hoti.
 * Unique Link & Identity: Har user ko ek unique HaproID aur custom profile link milta hai.
 * CV with QR Code & Profile Card: Isme aapko QR code ke saath digital profile card aur link-enabled CV milta hai, jise aap direct share kar sakte hain.
 * Google Visibility: Ye profiles Search Engine Optimization (SEO) friendly hoti hain, jisse ye Google search me bhi appear ho sakti hain.
Useful Use Cases
 * Students & Freelancers: Apne projects aur resume ko ek modern digital link me share karne ke liye.
 * Developers & Creators: Apne social profiles, portfolio, aur work experience ko showcase karne ke liye.
